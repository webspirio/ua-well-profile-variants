Pick a direction for the profile pages

Each phone is a live, scrollable prototype. Scroll inside it to see the hero animation. Drag down at the top to see the pull-down stretch. The controls change all three phones at once. «Профіль» switches between your own profile and another member's. «Хто дивиться» switches the viewer between an admin and a regular member. On someone else's profile, an admin also gets «Як адміністратор / Як учасник» inside the page. Sample people and photos are stock material, not real members.

ATelegram-native

Telegram's own profile header, in the UA WELL brand. A full-width 4:5 photo with the name bottom-left on a soft blurred band, and three frosted action tiles on the photo. Below it, Telegram-style grouped cards with the label above each value.

Motion
  • The photo moves up at half the scroll speed and never zooms.
  • The name shrinks to 57% and slides into the gap between Telegram's pills.
  • A frosted bar fades in once the photo has gone.
  • Pull down at the top and the photo stretches from its top edge, with no seam.
  • No photo: an orange colour cover with rings of brand marks that pull in and fade as you scroll.
Watch out for
  • The safest of the three. It feels native, but it borrows Telegram's identity more than it builds UA WELL's.
  • The heaviest use of blur (bar, tiles, band), so cheap Android phones need a solid fallback.

BContact Poster

An iOS Contact Poster. A full-screen portrait, the name huge in white over two lines, round glass buttons (Редагувати · Фото · Тема · Налаштування), and a light rounded sheet that rises over the photo.

Motion
  • As the sheet rises, the photo blurs and darkens progressively instead of sliding away.
  • The name and buttons fade out one by one.
  • A compact bar with a mini avatar and the name appears between the pills.
  • Pull down and the whole poster stretches, staying attached to the sheet.
Watch out for
  • The most emotional and premium of the three, but it depends most on the photo. With square photos the name lands on the chin, and bright photos need a stronger dim.
  • The prototype fakes each photo's brightness. The app doesn't store it today, so this needs either a new field at upload or measuring the colour in the browser.
  • The heaviest on GPU memory, because it keeps pre-blurred copies of the photo.

CEditorial

A calm magazine page. The photo is an inset portrait card with rounded corners and a warm shadow. Below it: a small «учасник з …» line, a big name, a dark «Редагувати профіль» button, and numbered sections with hairline openers. The bio has a hanging quote mark.

Motion
  • The identity sheet slides up over the photo card while the card dims.
  • Then a compact avatar and first name hand off into the top bar.
  • Pull down and the card grows into a full-bleed photo.
  • Sections fade up once as they enter the screen.
Watch out for
  • The least immersive of the three: the photo is framed, not edge to edge, until you pull down.
  • The fixed 4:5 frame crops landscape photos the hardest.
  • On a long profile, the many hairlines and cards can start to feel busy.
